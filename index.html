About

Hi, I'm Bruno.

I'm a director and cinematographer obsessed with light, texture, and the weight of a single frame.

Based in Lisbon, I work worldwide with brands, musicians, couples, and storytellers who believe visual craft matters. My approach blends documentary intimacy with cinematic precision — every project is built from genuine curiosity about the people and places involved.

I've had the privilege of working with indie labels, international NGOs, and boutique brands — always chasing the frame that makes someone feel something.
